AnalyX

Turn customer feedback and social comments into clear sentiment insights for your business.

Paste a public social post or product-review link to analyze up to 100 available comments or reviews.

YouTube public comments are available when its API is configured. Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and product-review sources require approved server-side access; availability depends on the platform and the content owner’s permissions.

Import historical comments or feedback from a CSV, Excel, or PDF file (up to 4 MB).

Sentiment charts use the multilingual DistilBERT classification; VADER is shown as a second opinion. Comments and reviews are retrieved only where an approved platform integration allows access.
